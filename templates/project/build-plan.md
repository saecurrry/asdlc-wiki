# Living build plan

## Scope, source facts and authority

Confirmed intent / exact versions, authorised local actions, exclusions and genuinely blocking choices. Proposed defaults are not agreement.

## Resume capsule

Current canonical revision/stage/dispatch (or not initialised), last verified result, pending findings/questions/approvals, and next permitted action. Do not infer completion from chat or checkboxes.

## Increments and delivery order

| Increment/story | Goal and acceptance | Depends on | Eligibility | Evidence / exact versions | Review | Human gate | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Detail the next ready increment and its validation; keep later work at outline depth until evidence supports refinement. Do not invent reliable estimates.

## Progress, decisions and learning

Record actual changes/checks, defects, blockers, remedies and links to decisions/lessons. Distinguish planned, executed, reviewed and human accepted.

## Recovery and invalidation

How to resume authoritative state, reconcile source changes and reject stale evidence. Preserve histories and input-version bindings. Stop for missing input or human gates; retry exhaustion is never completion.
